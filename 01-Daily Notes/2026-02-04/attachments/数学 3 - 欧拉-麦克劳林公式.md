$$S(n) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} f(i) = f(0) + f(1) + \dots + f(n)$$
有 $$S(x)-S(x-1)=f(x)$$
将 $S(x-1)$ 在 $x$ 处展开：
$$S(x-1) = S(x) - \frac{S'(x)}{1!} + \frac{S''(x)}{2!} - \frac{S'''(x)}{3!} + \dots$$

$$S(x) - \left( S(x) - S'(x) + \frac{1}{2}S''(x) - \frac{1}{6}S'''(x) + \dots \right) = f(x)$$
